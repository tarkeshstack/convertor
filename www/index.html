Your journey through settling in the UK

Settle In is a free, step-by-step guide covering: booking your first stay before you fly, airport arrival and taxis, opening a UK bank account, registering with the NHS, getting a SIM card, driving licence rules, sending money home, finding housing, buses and trains, schools, and the everyday apps (Amazon UK, Tesco, Citymapper and more) you'll use once you're settled.
